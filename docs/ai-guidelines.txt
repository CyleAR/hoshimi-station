Translate Japanese game text into natural, readable English following the rules below. Context, character relationships, and consistency of address and voice take priority over literal translation.

Source of truth: this guideline, docs/GLOSSARY.md (names, groups, places, fixed terms). The official English release is poor quality: it may be consulted only as a hint to disambiguate a term, and must never be copied verbatim.

[1. Output and preservation]

- Do not drop, merge, or split input sentences.
- Output only the translation, keeping the input order and format.
- Preserve `{user}`, `{threshold}` and every other `{...}` placeholder and all control codes exactly as in the source.
- Every placeholder in the source must appear in the translation the same number of times.
- Do not output the literal two-character string `\n`; break lines with real line breaks, keeping the source line-break count.
- Never introduce `[ ]`, `name=`, `clip=`, `hide=` or `thumbnial=` markup that is not in the source.
- No kana or kanji may remain in the output.
- No trailing spaces. Use ASCII quotes.
- Identical Japanese must give identical English.
- After translating, check control codes, placeholders, and missing sentences.

[2. Placeholders and the player]

- `{user}` is the Manager / Producer. English has no particles: leave `{user}` as is where it stands.
- `{user}くん`, `{user}君`, `{user}さん` -> `{user}` (honorific dropped unless the voice clearly needs it).
- Mei may address `{user}` in her own breezy way; follow the source's tone.
- `マネージャーさん` -> `Manager`.

[3. Punctuation and special characters]

- 、 -> `,`  。 -> `.`  ！ -> `!`  ？ -> `?`  ： -> `:`  ； -> `;`  ～ -> `~`
- `……` -> `...`
- Mid-sentence `――` -> `--`. Narration lines that begin with `――` keep the leading `――` (two U+2015).
- Long-vowel `ー` used for drawling in speech: render naturally (elongated vowel, or `~`).
- `！？` -> `?!`
- `♪` and `♡` are kept where the source has them.
- Lines wrapped in （…） keep their full-width parentheses.
- 「」 and 『』 -> double quotes `"`; nested quotes use single quotes `'`.
- Full-width space -> ASCII space; full-width alphanumerics -> ASCII.

[4. Honorifics and address]

- Honorifics are romanized and kept: -san, -chan, -kun, -senpai, -sama, as in the glossary (e.g. Saegusa-san, Gyogyotto-san).
- Do not convert honorifics into "Mr./Ms." or older-sister terms.
- Use the speaker's actual form of address from the source; keep it consistent for each speaker/listener pair.

Keep each character's voice consistent across lines.
